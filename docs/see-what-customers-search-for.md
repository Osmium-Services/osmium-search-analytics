**Search Analytics** records what customers type into your shop search.

## What you see

Totals for the last 30 days: **Total Searches**, **Unique Terms** and **Failed Searches**.

- **Top Searches** lists the most popular terms, how many times each was searched, and how many results it returned.
- **Failed Searches** lists terms that found nothing, with the count and when each was last searched.

## Fix a failed search

If customers search for a word you do not use, such as "trainers" when you sell "shoes", press **Add synonym** on the failed search. Enter what it **Maps to** and **Save**. From then on that search finds your products.
